01 The one thing worth reading twice

The core insight

Flippa, Acquire.com, Empire Flippers, and BizBuySell are all pure marketplaces. None of them has anything close to a 1M-weekly-reader audience or a 10,000-person trained buyer community feeding their platform. That's not a small edge. It's the one advantage none of them can copy or buy their way into.

But on contrarianthinking.co right now, BizScout isn't linked from the homepage at all. The audience that could out-distribute every competitor BizScout has is sitting one click away from the marketplace and currently zero clicks connected to it.

If BizScout's goal is genuinely to become #1 globally by end of 2026, this is the cheapest, fastest lever available, and it's already owned. Nothing new needs to be built or bought.

04 The competitive field, as it actually stands

FlippaOpen self-serve, $5K to $500K deals, 10% success fee. High traffic, lower buyer trust signal.
Acquire.comSaaS-focused, around 313K monthly visits. Strong in online and SaaS, weak in Main Street brick and mortar.
Empire FlippersVetted, higher trust, 15% fee. Slower process, better final pricing.
BizBuySellDominant sub $5M Main Street. Owned by LoopNet since 2004. No media or audience layer at all.

None of the four have an owned media audience. That's the gap BizScout can close that none of them can defend against.
